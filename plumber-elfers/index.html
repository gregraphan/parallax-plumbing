Plumbing in Elfers

Plumbing that knows Elfers

Most of Elfers went up between the 1970s and 90s, so polybutylene supply line is the pipe we replace most here, ahead of it failing. There's also a large share of manufactured homes with their own supply-line quirks, and many properties still run on septic, so we handle backups and drain-field issues too.

Elfers' defining feature is the Anclote River. It has flooded the community again and again, most recently to a roughly 200-year crest during Hurricane Milton, so freshwater flood cleanup, sewage backflow, and getting plumbing back online are calls we know well. The good news: Pasco County water out here tends to run a little softer than the city systems.
