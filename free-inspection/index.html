What we check

Everything in the 21-point inspection

This is not a quick glance. A licensed plumber walks your whole home, tests what matters, and writes it down, so you know the real condition of your plumbing and what, if anything, needs attention. Here is the full checklist.

Water heater age, condition, and TPR safety valve
Water pressure at the main
Every faucet and aerator
Every toilet: flush, fill, and hidden leaks
Under-sink supply lines and shutoff valves
Main water shutoff valve
All accessible and exposed piping
Pipe material check (poly, galvanized, cast-iron)
Drain flow at every fixture
Garbage disposal
Washing-machine hoses and supply box
Outdoor hose bibs and spigots
Water hardness and quality test
Signs of hidden or slab leaks
Sewer cleanout and main-line access
Expansion tank, where present
Backflow / pressure-reducing valve, where present
Caulk and seals at sinks, tubs, toilets
Water-heater sediment and flush check
Silent-leak check at the water meter
