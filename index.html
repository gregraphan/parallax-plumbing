Good to know

Questions homeowners ask

Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes. Parallax Plumbing is a Florida state-certified plumbing contractor, license CFC1431595, and we carry insurance on every job. You can verify our license any time at MyFloridaLicense.com.
Do you offer same-day service? +
Most repairs we can handle the same day you call, especially water heaters, leaks, and clogs. Booking online or calling early gives you the best shot at a same-day window.
How does your pricing work? +
We quote a flat, upfront price before any work begins, so you approve the cost first. No open-ended hourly billing and no surprises on the invoice.
What is your guarantee? +
Installs carry a one-year workmanship warranty, repairs 90 days, all under a 100% satisfaction promise. If something is not right, we come back and fix it.
What areas do you serve? +
New Port Richey, Port Richey, Trinity, Holiday, Hudson, Odessa, Elfers, and the surrounding West Pasco communities. Call us if you are nearby and unsure.
Do you handle plumbing emergencies? +
We prioritize emergency calls during business hours and respond as fast as we can. Call (727) 776-6199 and tell us what is happening, and we will get you scheduled quickly.
Can I finance a large repair? +
Yes. For bigger jobs like repipes and water heaters we offer financing through Wisetack, with approval in about a minute and rates as low as 0% APR for qualified customers.
