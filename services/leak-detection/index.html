What we do

Find the leak, fix the cause

Most leaks do their damage out of sight, inside a wall, under the slab, or underground between the meter and the house. We use pressure testing and acoustic and moisture tools to pin down where the water is actually escaping, so the repair is targeted instead of exploratory.

Catching it early matters here. Florida humidity and a warm slab turn a small hidden leak into mold and rot fast, and a slab leak left alone only gets more expensive. If your bill or your water meter says something is off, it usually is.

When to call

Signs you have a hidden leak

A water bill that jumped

A bill that climbs with no change in use almost always means water is escaping somewhere you cannot see.

Damp spots, mold, or a musty smell

Warm walls, stains, buckling floors, or a musty smell point to water leaking behind the surface.

The meter moves with everything off

If your water meter keeps ticking when every fixture is shut off, you have a leak on the line.

Good to know

Leak Detection questions, answered

How do you find a leak without breaking walls? +
We start with pressure tests and acoustic and moisture tools to narrow down the location, then open only the small area we need. The goal is to find it first and cut once, not go hunting through your drywall.
What is a slab leak, and is it serious? +
A slab leak is a pipe leaking under the concrete foundation. It is worth taking seriously, because left alone it can undermine the slab and drive up your water bill, but caught early it is very fixable.
My water bill spiked. Does that mean a leak? +
Often, yes. A sudden jump with no change in how much water you use is one of the most common signs of a hidden leak. We can test and tell you for sure.
Do you repair the leak the same visit? +
Many leaks we can locate and repair in one trip. Slab and main-line leaks sometimes take a little more, and we give you a clear plan and price before we start.
