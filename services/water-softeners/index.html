What we do

Soft, clean water through the whole house

Hard water is not just an annoyance. The dissolved minerals in Floridan-aquifer water scale up water heaters, stiffen laundry, spot your glasses and fixtures, and slowly narrow your pipes. A properly sized softener treats it before it reaches any of that.

Some homes need more than softening. Well-served properties around Odessa and Spring Hill often deal with iron, a sulfur smell, or sediment too, so we test what is actually in your water and match the right softener or filtration system to it.

When to call

Signs West Pasco water is getting to your home

Spots, film, and stiff laundry

White spots on glasses, film in the shower, and scratchy laundry are the everyday signs of hard water.

Water heaters that die early

Scale builds up inside the tank and shortens its life, so hard water quietly costs you heaters.

Iron stains or a sulfur smell

Rusty staining or a rotten-egg smell, common on well water here, calls for filtration, not just softening.
