When to call

Signs of a drain or sewer problem

More than one slow drain

When several drains slow down at once, the clog is usually in the main line, not a single fixture.

Gurgling or a sewage smell

Gurgling toilets, bad odors, or water backing up in a tub point to a main-line or sewer blockage.

The same drain keeps clogging

A drain that backs up every few weeks usually has roots, grease, or corroded cast iron that only a camera will reveal.

Good to know

Drain Cleaning questions, answered

Why does my drain keep clogging? +
Repeat clogs almost always have a root cause, tree roots, grease buildup, or a pipe that has corroded or sagged. That is why we camera the line instead of just snaking it and hoping.
What is hydro-jetting? +
Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls clean, cutting through grease and roots that a cable snake only pokes a hole in. It is the more thorough option for a line that keeps backing up.
Do you use a camera? +
Yes. For recurring or main-line issues we run a camera to see exactly what is happening and where, so you get a real diagnosis and not a guess.
Can you fix a broken sewer line without digging up my yard? +
Often, yes. Trenchless methods let us repair or reline many sewer lines with minimal digging, which saves your landscaping and a lot of cleanup.
