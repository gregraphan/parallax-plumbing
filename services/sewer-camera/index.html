What we do

Eyes on the line before anyone digs

When to call

Signs of a sewer line problem

Every drain is slow at once

When the whole house drains slowly or backs up together, the trouble is in the main sewer line.

Backups that keep returning

A main line that clears then backs up again usually has roots or a broken section a camera will reveal.

Sewer smell or soggy spots in the yard

Odors indoors or wet, extra-green patches in the yard can mean the sewer line is cracked or leaking.

Good to know

Sewer & Camera questions, answered

What does a sewer camera inspection show? +
It shows the inside of your sewer line on video, so we can see roots, cracks, sags, or corrosion and know the exact spot and depth. You see it too, so the diagnosis is not a guess.
Should I get a sewer inspection before buying a home? +
For any older home, it is one of the smartest inspections you can get. Sewer repairs are expensive, and knowing the condition of the line before you buy can save you a lot.
Why do my drains keep backing up even after snaking? +
Snaking punches a hole through the clog but leaves the cause. If roots or a broken pipe are the real problem, it comes back. A camera finds the cause so we can actually fix it.
