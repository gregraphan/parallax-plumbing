What we do

Eyes on the line before anyone digs

When a whole house drains slowly or a main line keeps backing up, the answer is underground where you cannot see it. We run a camera through the sewer line to find the exact spot and cause, a root intrusion, a collapse, a belly, or corroded cast iron, so the repair fixes the real problem.

Older New Port Richey and Holiday homes are often on cast-iron sewers that rust and crack from the inside and pull in tree roots. Once we know the exact problem and where it is, we repair or replace only the section that needs it.

When to call

Signs of a sewer line problem

Every drain is slow at once

When the whole house drains slowly or backs up together, the trouble is in the main sewer line.

Backups that keep returning

A main line that clears then backs up again usually has roots or a broken section a camera will reveal.

Sewer smell or soggy spots in the yard

Odors indoors or wet, extra-green patches in the yard can mean the sewer line is cracked or leaking.
