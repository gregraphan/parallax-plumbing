Good to know

Gas & Commercial questions, answered

Are you licensed to do gas work? +
Yes. Parallax is a Florida state-certified plumbing contractor, license CFC1431595, and gas piping is done to code with the required permits and pressure testing.
Can you run a gas line for a range, pool heater, or generator? +
Yes. We size, run, and test gas lines for ranges, dryers, water heaters, pool and patio heaters, and standby generators.
Do you do commercial plumbing? +
We do. Parallax handles commercial new-construction and remodel plumbing across West Pasco, from rough-in through fixtures.
I think I smell gas. What should I do? +
Leave the building first, and do not flip switches or light anything. From a safe spot, call your gas utility, and once it is safe we can find and repair the line. Your safety comes before the repair.
