Good to know

Faucets & Fixtures questions, answered

Is a small job like one faucet worth your time? +
Yes. We take the small fixture jobs on purpose, because they are how a lot of homeowners meet us. No job is too small to do right.
Should I repair or replace a fixture? +
It depends on the age and the part. A worn cartridge in a good faucet is an easy repair, while a corroded or dated fixture is often better replaced. We tell you which way makes sense.
Can you install a fixture I already bought? +
Absolutely. Bring your own faucet, toilet, or disposal and we will install it correctly. We will also flag it early if something is not going to fit or work.
Do you handle the plumbing for a bathroom remodel? +
Yes. We set sinks, faucets, tubs, toilets, and valves for remodels, on your schedule and to code, and coordinate with the rest of your project.
