When to call for fixture work

A drip that will not quit

A dripping faucet or a running toilet wastes water every day and usually means a worn valve or seal.

Wobbly, slow, or leaking fixtures

A rocking toilet, a slow-filling tank, or a puddle under the sink are small problems that get bigger if ignored.

Planning a kitchen or bath update? Getting the plumbing set right is what makes the new fixtures work and last.
