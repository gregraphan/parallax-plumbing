What we do

Tank, tankless, and everything that goes with them

A water heater is the appliance you only notice when it quits. We fix the ones worth saving, a bad thermostat, a burnt element, a failed valve, and we replace the ones that cannot be, sized to how your household actually uses hot water.

West Pasco runs on hard Floridan-aquifer water, which scales up a tank and shortens its life. So we also flush systems, pair in softeners, and add expansion tanks and drain pans so the next heater lasts longer than the last.

When to call

Signs your water heater needs a look

No hot water, or it runs out fast

Cold showers or a tank that cannot keep up usually means a failing element, thermostat, or an undersized unit.

Water pooling at the base

A wet spot under the tank is often a cracked tank or a failed valve, and it rarely gets better on its own.

Rusty water or a rumbling tank

Popping, rumbling, or discolored hot water points to sediment and scale from our hard local water.

Good to know

Water Heaters questions, answered

Should I repair or replace my water heater? +
If the tank itself is leaking or it is past about ten years old, replacement is usually the better value. Bad elements, thermostats, and valves on a newer tank are worth repairing. We tell you straight which one you are looking at.
Is tankless worth it here? +
Tankless gives you endless hot water and a longer lifespan, and it frees up floor space. It costs more up front, so it pays off best for larger households or anyone tired of running out. We size and quote both so you can compare.
Can you replace a water heater the same day? +
Most standard tank swaps we can do the same day you call. Tankless and gas conversions sometimes need a little more planning, but we give you a firm timeline up front.
Do you haul away the old unit? +
Yes. Removal and disposal of the old water heater is included, and we leave the space cleaner than we found it.
