Questions Port Richey homeowners ask

Should I replace my polybutylene pipe before it fails? +
In Port Richey's 1970s-to-90s homes, yes. Polybutylene supply line fails without warning and can flood a home. Replacing it with PEX or copper ahead of time is far cheaper than water-damage cleanup.
Does living on a canal affect my plumbing? +
It does. Salt air and brackish water along the Cotee River and the US 19 canal streets corrode pipes, fittings, and water heaters faster than inland, so those homes need closer attention.
